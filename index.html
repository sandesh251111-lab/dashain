The story behind the celebration

A Festival Rooted in Victory

Dashain, also known as Bada Dashain, is one of Nepal's most important Hindu festivals. It brings together religious devotion, family traditions, and hope for a brighter future.

01

The Victory of Goddess Durga

In a widely celebrated Hindu story, the goddess Durga battles the buffalo demon Mahishasura, whose growing power threatens the world. After a fierce struggle, Durga defeats him. Her victory represents the triumph of good over evil and the power of courage, justice, and divine strength. The first nine days of Dashain are associated with the worship of Durga in many traditions.

02

The Meaning of Vijaya Dashami

Vijaya Dashami is the tenth day of the festival. In another important Hindu tradition, it is associated with Lord Rama's victory over Ravana. In Nepal, elders traditionally place tika and jamara on younger family members and offer blessings. It is a time for reconciliation, respect, gratitude, and gathering with loved ones.

03

Ten Days of Celebration

The festival unfolds over several days, including Ghatasthapana, the worship of the goddess during Navaratri, Fulpati, Maha Ashtami, Maha Navami, Vijaya Dashami, and the following days of family visits and blessings. Customs vary between communities and households, reflecting Nepal's rich cultural diversity.

04

A Tradition Passed Through Generations

For generations, Dashain has been a time for relatives to reunite, families to share meals, people to travel home, and communities to celebrate together. Tika, jamara, swings, new clothes, festive foods, and flying kites are familiar parts of the season for many Nepalis. The festival continues to carry memories and meaning across generations.

Why it matters: Beyond the stories of victory, Dashain is a reminder of family bonds, kindness, renewal, and the hope that goodness will prevail. Traditions differ across Nepal's many communities.
The heart of Dashain

Traditions We Treasure

The small rituals, familiar sounds, and shared moments that make Dashain feel like home.

🌾

Tika & Jamara

Elders offer blessings to younger family members with tika and jamara, expressing wishes for happiness, health, wisdom, and prosperity.

🪁

Kites in the Sky

Colourful kites brighten the sky during Dashain. For many people, they evoke childhood memories, open skies, playful competition, and the excitement of the festive season.

🏡

Coming Home

Many families travel to reunite, share meals, receive blessings, and reconnect. For those far away, a message or call can carry a little feeling of home.

🎋

Dashain Swings

Traditional bamboo swings, often called ping, are part of the festive landscape in many places. They bring laughter, excitement, and memories shared between generations.

🪔

Prayer & Gratitude

Worship, family rituals, and offerings express devotion and gratitude. For many, the season is also an opportunity to reflect and begin again with hope.

❤️

Love & Blessings

Greetings, gifts, visits, and kind words help strengthen relationships. Even a small message can remind someone that they are remembered and valued.
